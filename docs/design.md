# Pogo-stick based game

_Note theme connection: SHIFT = "shift weight/gear/position"; shift key to power-bounce._

Aim: Race around a track on a pogo stick, which you can rotate underneath you to control your speed & direction indirectly.

Mechanics: Classical 3D physics with a pogo stick which gives a rebound force from the ground while in contact - both along the line of action of the stick, and probably a bit of friction too for when the user is giving a "rotate" control while the stick is in contact.

Controls: Rotate-left/rotate-right/power-bounce. Rotation commands rotate the pogo stick at any time, and power-bounce increases the rebound force from the ground, with a larger increase when released while the pogo is in contact.

Challenge: Mastering the controls to pull off cool moves. Achieving faster lap times and beating AI opponents. Progressing to more twitchy / exciting pogos.

Graphics: Low-poly toon/cel shaded, aiming for a funny/toy-like look. Fog to help gauge depth. Blob shadow for the pogo rider. Minimal HUD for lap count, timer, gates.
